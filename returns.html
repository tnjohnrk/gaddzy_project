Introduction

At Gaddzy, we want every transaction to be smooth and transparent. This Return Policy outlines the conditions under which a return, cancellation, or reversal of a sell order is accepted. Please read this carefully before completing any transaction on our platform.

01. Eligibility for Return

A return or cancellation request may be raised in the following situations:

Price Discrepancies

The valuation price offered during pickup differs significantly from the online quote without a valid diagnostic reason.

Payment Delays

The device has been successfully collected by our logistics agent, but payment is not sent to you within the committed timeframe.

Inspection Damage

The device is returned to you in a physically damaged state following our technical inspection sweeps.

Order Cancellations

You cancel your sell booking request at any point before our pickup agent arrives at your specified address.

02. Non-Returnable Situations

Returns or cancellations will not be accepted in the following cases:

The device has already been formally processed at our warehouse and payment has been successfully dispatched to your bank account or UPI handle.
The final physical assessment reveals that the actual device condition, lock profiles, or storage specifications differ from the details you submitted online.
The device has been successfully unlocked and all personal user configurations have been swept clean as part of our secure data recycling protocol.
The cancellation request is raised more than 48 hours after the physical doorstep pickup was completed.

03. How to Raise a Return Request

To initiate a return or dispute, please follow these steps:

Log in to your GADDZY account and navigate to My Orders.

Select the relevant order and click "Raise a Dispute".

Describe the issue clearly and attach supporting photos of your device if applicable.

Our legal compliance desk will review your claim details within 2–3 business days.

Alternatively, contact us directly at Gaddzyonline@gmail.com or call our customer desk at +91 73582 06525.

04. Device Return Process

If your return request is approved, GADDZY will coordinate for the device to be safely couriered back to your registered doorstep address within 5–7 business days. The device will be returned in the exact same condition it was picked up. We bear the entire cost of return shipping for all approved return cases.

05. Payment Reversals

If payment was made to you and a subsequent verification dispute is found valid (e.g. device is reported lost/stolen after payment), GADDZY reserves the right to reverse, hold, or adjust the payment. In such cases, we will notify you at least 24 hours in advance and provide a full explanation.

06. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Return Policy at any time. Updated versions will be posted on this page with the revised effective date. Continued use of our services constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
