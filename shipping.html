Introduction

At Gaddzy, we offer a completely doorstep pickup experience — you don't need to ship anything. Our trained pickup agents come to your location, inspect the device, and process the payment on the spot. This page explains how our pickup and logistics process works.

01. Pickup Service Coverage

We currently offer doorstep pickup in the following areas:

Chennai and surrounding districts (Tamil Nadu)

Select PIN codes within our serviceable zones

You can check if your PIN code is serviceable on our website during order placement. We are continuously expanding our coverage areas.

02. Scheduling a Pickup

Once you submit your device details and accept the quoted price, you will be prompted to select a pickup slot.

Available slots are typically same-day or next-day, subject to agent availability in your area. You will receive a confirmation SMS/email with the agent's name and contact number before pickup. The agent will arrive within the selected time window.

03. What to Prepare Before Pickup

To ensure a smooth pickup experience, please complete these steps before the agent arrives:

Backup Data

Back up all your personal data (photos, contacts, apps, calendar logs, etc.) to an external server or cloud account.

Factory Reset

Perform a complete factory reset and remove your Google, Apple, or iCloud profile locks before handing over the device.

Invoice & Accessories

Keep the device's original box, charging accessories, and invoice invoice handy (not mandatory, but adds bonus value).

Charge Battery

Ensure the device is charged to at least 20% to allow our agent to run diagnostics properly.

Lastly, please remember to remove all SIM cards and external micro-SD storage slots before the handover.

04. Device Inspection at Pickup

Our agent will conduct a physical inspection at your doorstep to verify the device's condition against the details you submitted. This includes:

We test the screen quality, check for dead pixels, screen burn-ins, color shifts, and verify the touch responsiveness across the display.
We verify the battery health capacity, charging port connection, camera lenses (front/rear), speaker volume, and microphone clarity.
We log any minor scratches, back panel dents, body cracks, or frame bends to assess structural wear.
We cross-check the device's unique IMEI number against cellular blacklist databases to prevent trade in of lost or stolen items.

If the device condition matches the submitted details, the quoted price is honoured. If there is a discrepancy, a revised offer will be made. You have the right to accept or decline it.

05. Payment After Pickup

Payment is processed via Bank Transfer (NEFT/IMPS) or UPI. Once the device is handed over and the final price is agreed upon, payment is initiated within 24–48 hours. You will receive a payment confirmation notification once the transfer is complete.

06. Rescheduling or Cancelling a Pickup

You can reschedule or cancel a pickup up to 2 hours before the scheduled slot through your account under My Orders. Repeated cancellations may result in your account being flagged or restricted.

If our agent arrives and you are unavailable, the slot will be marked as missed. You may rebook another slot after 24 hours.

07. Device Safety During Pickup

Our agents are trained professionals. However, if you feel any issue with the pickup process, you may decline handing over the device at any point before the handover is complete. Once the device is handed over and the job sheet is signed, the transaction is considered final.
