Introduction

Welcome to Gaddzy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (www.gaddzy.com) and use our services for buying and selling mobile devices, laptops, and tablets.

01. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our platform:

Personal & Account Data

  • Personal details (Name, Email, Phone)
  • Shipping and billing addresses
  • Encrypted passwords & account preferences
  • Order history & payment records
  • Pickup or delivery dispatch details

Device & Usage Data

  • Device Brand, Model, Storage & Condition
  • IMEI Number & uploaded hardware photos
  • Web activity logs & time spent on site
  • Search queries, device metadata & browser type
  • Cookies & tracking pixels data

02. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to manage your trades, verify device profiles, and provide a premium, friction-free re-commerce experience:

Submit Information

Generate accurate price quotes based on the specific condition, model capacity, and parameters you input.

Verification & Order Setup

Process buy/sell transactions, verify serial numbers, and coordinate doorstep delivery/pickup dispatches.

Support & Communications

Keep you updated on order statuses, send automated payment receipts, and resolve customer support queries.

Security & Fraud Check

Decline unauthorized access, prevent recommerce abuse, and protect users against fraud.

Marketing & Promos

Distribute trade-in discount vouchers and newsletters (strictly subject to your explicit opt-in preferences).

Compliance

Keep legal accounting entries, resolve disputes, and comply with state legal regulations.

03. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

We share relevant details with trusted operational partners (payment gateways, logistics providers, cloud server networks) who help us operate our platform under strict data protection agreements.
We may disclose information if required by law, court order, search warrant, or government authority to comply with legal compliance duties.
We reserve the right to share information to protect the rights, property, safety, or assets of Gaddzy, our users, our staff, or the public.
In the event of an organizational merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of corporate assets, customer data may be transferred to the acquiring legal entity.

04. Cookies Policy

Essential Cookies

Purpose Required for basic site navigation, account sessions, and secure forms to function properly.
Disableable No

Disableable Yes

05. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption, secure servers, and access controls to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. While we take every reasonable precaution, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Security Notice

We strictly encrypt transactional exchanges. However, we advise users to perform system wipes and unlink active cloud drives before shipping devices to protect system files.

06. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When your data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

07. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access & Portability

Correction & Deletion

Opt-out & Consent Withdrawal
